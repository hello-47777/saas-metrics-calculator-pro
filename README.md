📐 Metric Definitions
MRR — Monthly Recurring Revenue
Definition: The total predictable revenue generated by all active subscriptions in a single month.
Formula: MRR = Σ (Monthly Subscription Revenue per User)

Churn Rate
Definition: The percentage of customers or revenue lost during a specific period. It is the "Silent Killer" of SaaS growth.

Excellent: < 2%

Healthy: 2%–5%

Critical: > 5%

LTV:CAC Ratio — The Gold Standard
Definition: Measures the relationship between the lifetime value of a customer and the cost to acquire them.

Ideal (3x - 5x): Healthy and sustainable growth.

Dangerous (< 1x): Losing money on every customer acquired.
